## This integration artifact (what it does and does not do)

- `mission_agent/mission_control/frank_ca_f02_alignment.py` is a pure function accepting **already computed** F01 follow eligibility, C02 CA research report, F02 verified research positions, and selected exact Mint. It never loads private raw RPC, calls a URL, writes DB, initiates new CA work, decides BUY or quotes.
- Result has separate `f01`, `c02`, `f02` sections. `combined_trade_permission=false` **always**; `F01_ELIGIBLE` is a record of *F01's own frozen filtered decision*, not a permission invented by the joint view. F02 quantities are explicitly archived and hidden on stale/failure, partial source or conflicting latest attempt metadata. A fresh F02 observed zero vs F01 eligible triggers a **review flag**, not a SELL assertion. Missing Mint outside frozen F02 32 says `OUTSIDE_FROZEN_TARGET_ROSTER`, not zero. 
- C02 exact mint identity is displayed **only** when chain/exact mint match and upstream metadata is verified without conflict. Narrative status is copied from actual `assessment.narrative_status` when the report really provides it, otherwise `UNVERIFIED`; it never invents X/official promotion or trust a name alone.
- `tests/test_frank_f01_c02_f02_alignment.py` runs against code-only inputs, including stale F02, partial latest, exact-UTC metadata mismatch, duplicate records, zero observed vs null, same-name different exact Mint and uncertain narrative. 
- `integration_tests/test_frank_f01_c02_f02_exact_contract.py` is **not** in the base F02-only test suite. The CI combined job explicitly merges the original reviewed F01 and C02 branches in a disposable checkout and runs a synthetic dual-program original-wire/real read-only Sidecar + real `partition_candidates` + real `reconcile_identity` against one exact target Mint. It rechecks no SELL revival, no cross-Mint inheritance and no stale archived positive display. The fixture can create temporary synthetic research files and mock RPC responses but cannot contact Solana mainnet or mutate a user's Mac/production data.
- A first combined CI run showed a **genuine integration issue**: F02 emits `0.000000` for a valid zero observed balance, while joint adapter initially required the literal `"0"`. That incorrectly hid legitimate observed-zero as unknown. It was repaired with exact Decimal zero classification and a permanent regression; do not erase initial failed CI history.
