## Participation / farming

Late farming is less attractive than early farming because point emissions have halved multiple times.

Best current risk-adjusted routes:
1. Existing historical points: preserve them.
2. DUSD holding in Perps/Vault: low activity but exposed to DUSD/protocol risks.
3. Maker Points: resting orders can earn without fill, but real orders can be executed.
4. Community Maker Yield / Maker Uptime: strongest direct token-allocation signal, but requires tight two-sided quoting and real fill/inventory risk.
5. Trading solely to farm points is not attractive unless the user would trade anyway.

Do not spend large capital purely on StandX points until:
- tokenomics;
- point-to-token conversion;
- snapshot;
- initial circulation;
- claim terms
are published.
