## Important distinction: order-book simulation is not a trade

Original Stage90 can check exact Binance USD-M book raw JSON and SHA, exchange update timestamps, bids/asks, depth walk and source-described taker fees. It correctly reports `NOTIONAL_UNKNOWN` with no user-provided quote amount. Even a fully specified synthetic fixture returns `SNAPSHOT_COST_EVIDENCE_ONLY`, not a proven future exit or profit. Stage119 additionally fails closed on absent raw identity/mapping/market/fee source bytes, SHA mismatch, future source timestamps and nonmatching event IDs. A user text field saying a record came from Binance is **not authenticated provenance**, and a syntactically valid SHA alone cannot prove a crypto token or true fill.

Stage119 forward CLI does **not** create real one-time captures. The original Stage99 approved three one-shot fixed-window market snapshots remain unchanged. It accepts their actual verified bytes and Stage116 finished replay only after they become available; then optional real event-time order-book / identity / mapping / fee records may be evaluated. Historical stale data, a quote captured after the decision beyond allowed latency, wrong symbol or unauthenticated source cannot be upgraded to BUY.
