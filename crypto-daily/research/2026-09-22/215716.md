### EU stablecoin reserve policy
- discovery_time: 2026-09-22 21:57 Asia/Bangkok
- topic: MiCA stablecoin reserve rules
- why_it_matters: ECB and EU national central banks are recommending removal of MiCA's fixed bank-deposit reserve requirement for stablecoin issuers, replacing it with a liquidity/maturity approach. This could materially change reserve management and bank funding exposure for EU stablecoins.
- confirmed_facts: Reuters reports the current rule requires 30% of reserves in bank deposits, or 60% for major issuers. Central banks propose instead requiring a portion of reserves in assets maturing within one to five working days. They also continue to warn against multi-issuance structures spanning EU and non-EU entities.
- source: Reuters, 2026-09-22, https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/ecb-eu-central-banks-oppose-stablecoin-bank-deposit-rule-2026-09-22/
