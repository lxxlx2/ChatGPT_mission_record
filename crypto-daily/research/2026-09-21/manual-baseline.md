## Polymarket token vs equity / Pre-Access structure

- Polymarket official Help Center dated 2026-06-24 says it has not announced plans for an airdrop or token generation event.
- Reuters reported Polymarket hired Warren Jenson as its first CFO on 2026-09-10. This is material corporate-finance/IPO-readiness evidence, although it does not prove an IPO timetable.
- Binance Pre-Access FAQ dated 2026-09-20 says Pre-Access tokens are third-party tokenized assets that may give indirect exposure to a private firm. Binance Wallet is only the self-custodial interface/technical access point and does not issue, sponsor or operate the token/campaign.
- Binance's broader Pre-IPO FAQ says these assets do not represent direct ownership of underlying company shares and do not provide shareholder rights.
- Therefore any pPOLY/Polymarket private-company exposure must be separated from an official POLY token. The existence of private-company exposure strengthens an equity/SPV pathway thesis but does not by itself prove an official Polymarket partnership or make a future token impossible.
