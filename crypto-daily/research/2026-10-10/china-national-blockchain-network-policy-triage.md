This confirms a CPC Central Committee + State Council joint policy with 19 measures in five domains, but its English summary does not itself quote the blockchain-network clause.

These quote the policy call for both national blockchain and nationwide integrated computing networks, and mention data rights and industrial digitization. - Also official English local policy, Aug 27: https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-Policies/20260827/0afad4cc7a124e24ac915ef0224e1d9b.html mentions expanding Shanghai's hub within the national blockchain network.

## Factual limits and investment significance
CONFIRMED: An Oct 9 high-level technology/economic policy reaffirmed construction of a national blockchain network in an industrial/data infrastructure package. Prior 15th Five-Year Plan already mentioned it.
NOT VERIFIED: finalized technical design, whether this is a newly launched public L1, permissionless access, implementer/operator, budget, construction deadline, new token/tokenomics, specific publicly tradable beneficiaries, or a relaxation of domestic crypto exchange restrictions.
Interpretation: policy favors national digital industrial infrastructure, data identity/data circulation and government-managed standards. It is NOT evidence of domestic permissionless crypto trading legalization or any cryptocurrency's immediate fundamental cash flows. No direct buy recommendation.
