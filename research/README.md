# Crypto Research Library

Updated: 2026-09-26

Human-facing Crypto research is organized into four research categories.

- `projects/`: project due diligence, ICO/public sale, protocol/product records.
- `tokens/`: secondary market, tokenomics, derivatives, squeeze/buyback/liquidity analysis.
- `memes/`: meme-specific narrative, launchpad, social-flow and trading research.
- `nfts/`: mint, collection, creator, rarity, listing and secondary-market research.

Monitoring/task mechanics stay outside this library in the existing automation roots and `docs/MONITORING/`.

Operational current positions may remain under `crypto-300-profit-mission/positions/`; research files should link to those operational authorities when relevant.
