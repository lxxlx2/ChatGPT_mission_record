As of **2026-11-06**. Scope: confirm which older releases can actually be attributed to PocketPad founder Hunt (`@huntclubhero`) or an identifiable collaborator; compare **historical mint price to verified historical peak** only where both are available. This is historical due diligence, **not** a live Pocket mint/marketplace or contract audit. No wallet operations, automation or trading.
